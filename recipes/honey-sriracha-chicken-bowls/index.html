Honey-Sriracha Chicken Bowls

Sticky honey-garlic-sriracha chicken thighs over jasmine rice with roasted broccoli.

Main DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Total40 min
Servings7 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 5 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Cook jasmine rice according to package directions.
  2. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss broccoli with 1 tbsp olive oil and black pepper. Roast 18–22 minutes.
  3. Heat remaining olive oil in a large skillet. Cook chicken 7–9 minutes, until it reaches 165°F.
  4. Whisk honey, soy sauce, sriracha, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger.
  5. Add sauce to chicken and simmer 2–3 minutes. Mix cornstarch with water, stir into skillet, and simmer until thickened.
  6. Serve chicken and sauce over rice with broccoli. Sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired.

Recipe Notes

  • For less heat, start with 2 tbsp sriracha and add more to taste.
  • Prepare sauce ahead and refrigerate separately from raw chicken.
  • Refrigerate leftovers promptly and use within 3–4 days.
