Classic Green Bean Casserole

The familiar single-batch French's green bean casserole with canned green beans, cream of mushroom soup, and crispy fried onions.

Side Dish
Prep10 min
Cook35 min
Total45 min
Yield6 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Combine condensed soup, milk, soy sauce, and black pepper.
  3. Fold in drained green beans and about ⅔ cup crispy onions.
  4. Transfer to a 1½–2-quart casserole dish and bake 25–30 minutes until hot and bubbling.
  5. Top with remaining crispy onions and bake another 5 minutes until golden.
