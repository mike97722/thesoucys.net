Dutch Oven Crispy Pork Carnitas Tacos

Citrus-garlic pork shoulder braised in a Dutch oven, shredded and broiled until crisp, served with tortillas and rice.

Main DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook4 hr 15 min
Total4 hr 35 min
Servings8 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 8 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F. Cut pork shoulder into large 3–4-inch chunks and pat dry.
  2. Combine cumin, oregano, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper; coat pork evenly.
  3. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown pork in batches on several sides.
  4. Add quartered onion, garlic, orange juice, lime juice, chicken broth, and bay leaves.
  5. Cover and braise in the oven 3–4 hours, until pork is fork-tender and shreds easily.
  6. Remove pork and shred with two forks. Discard bay leaves and onion pieces.
  7. Skim excess fat from the braising liquid and reduce it on the stovetop if watery. Toss shredded pork with enough liquid to keep moist.
  8. Spread pork on a sheet pan and broil 3–5 minutes until edges are crispy. Toss with a little more braising liquid; repeat if desired.
  9. Cook jasmine rice according to package directions.
  10. Warm corn tortillas and serve with crispy pork, rice, salsa, avocado, and optional toppings.

Recipe Notes

Use pork shoulder rather than pork loin. Refrigerate after braising if preparing ahead, and reheat thoroughly before crisping. Keep cheese and sour cream separate; verify packaged ingredients for dairy and almond allergens.
