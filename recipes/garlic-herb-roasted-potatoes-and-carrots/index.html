Garlic Herb Roasted Potatoes & Carrots

Golden baby Yukon potatoes and sweet roasted carrots tossed with garlic, rosemary, and thyme.

Dairy-freeAlmond-freeNo raw onions
Prep15 min
Cook40 min
Total55 min
Servings6 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line two large rimmed sheet pans with parchment if desired.
  2. Pat potatoes and carrots dry. Halve potatoes so pieces are similar in size.
  3. Toss potatoes and carrots with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, optional paprika, salt, and black pepper. Spread in a single layer on the pans with space between pieces.
  4. Roast 20 minutes, then turn the vegetables and rotate pans. Roast another 10–15 minutes until potatoes are golden and carrots are tender.
  5. Add minced garlic during the last 5 minutes of roasting, tossing gently, so it becomes fragrant without burning.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning. Finish with parsley and a little lemon juice if desired. Serve hot.

Family kitchen notes

For a more caramelized finish, avoid crowding the pans. This recipe uses olive oil rather than butter and pairs especially well with rotisserie chicken.
