Garlic-Ginger Beef & Broccoli

Tender sirloin and crisp-tender broccoli coated in a savory garlic-ginger stir-fry sauce.

Dairy-freeAlmond-freeNo raw onions
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Total40 min
Servings8 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Slice sirloin thinly against the grain. Toss with 1 tablespoon cornstarch and black pepper.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, beef broth, brown sugar, rice vinegar, 2 tablespoons cornstarch, and cold water until smooth.
  3. Steam or blanch broccoli 2–3 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender; drain thoroughly.
  4. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat. Sear beef in batches, adding more oil as needed, for 1–2 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  5. Reduce heat to medium. Add garlic and ginger; stir for 30 seconds until fragrant without burning.
  6. Whisk sauce again, pour into skillet, and simmer 1–2 minutes until thickened. Return beef and broccoli to pan and toss until coated and beef is cooked to your preference.
  7. Serve immediately over rice if desired, with green onion tops.

Family kitchen notes

Use sirloin as planned. For an almond-free and dairy-free meal, verify labels on soy sauce and broth. Avoid raw onion bulbs; optional green onion tops may be omitted.
