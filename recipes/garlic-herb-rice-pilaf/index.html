Garlic Herb Rice Pilaf

Fluffy rice pilaf with toasted vermicelli, garlic, thyme, parsley, and chicken broth. A flavorful dairy-free side for blackened chicken and family dinners.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Servings5 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 5 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil and plant-based butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add vermicelli or broken spaghetti and cook, stirring frequently, for 2–3 minutes until golden brown.
  3. Stir in the rice and toast for about 2 minutes. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Carefully add chicken broth, kosher salt, black pepper, dried parsley, thyme, and bay leaf. Stir and bring to a boil.
  5. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 15–18 minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Avoid lifting the lid during cooking.
  6. Remove the pan from the heat and let stand, covered, for 10 minutes.
  7. Discard the bay leaf and fluff with a fork. Stir in optional fresh parsley and lemon juice before serving.

Recipe Notes

Optional Cajun variation: Sauté ½ cup diced red bell pepper and ½ cup diced celery until softened before adding the vermicelli; add ½ tsp Cajun seasoning with the broth and herbs. This is especially good alongside grilled blackened chicken.
