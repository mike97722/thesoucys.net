Cajun Rotisserie Chicken Pasta

Tender rotisserie chicken, colorful peppers, and penne tossed in a bold, silky Cajun sauce made with coconut cream. Big flavor without dairy, and ready in about 40 minutes.

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook penne until just al dente according to package directions. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Toss the shredded rotisserie chicken with 1 tablespoon of the Cajun seasoning. Set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Add the bell peppers and diced onion; cook 6–8 minutes, stirring, until softened and the onion is fully cooked.
  4. Stir in garlic, tomato paste, smoked paprika, and remaining 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Pour in chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits. Stir in coconut cream and optional Worcestershire sauce. Simmer gently for 4–5 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is smooth and slightly thickened.
  6. Add seasoned rotisserie chicken and simmer 2–3 minutes until heated through. Stir in lemon juice. Taste before adding salt because Cajun seasoning and rotisserie chicken may already be salty.
  7. Add drained pasta and toss to coat. Loosen with reserved pasta water, a splash at a time, until glossy and creamy. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.

Family kitchen notes

For extra heat, add cayenne a pinch at a time. Choose an unsweetened coconut cream without added sugar; the seasoning, tomato paste and lemon help balance the coconut flavor. Check packaged seasoning and chicken labels for dairy and almond cross-contact when needed.
