The Soucy’s Macaroni Salad

A family-style ditalini macaroni salad with hard-boiled eggs, diced cheddar, sweet pickles, and mayonnaise.

Side Dish
Prep2 hr
Cook30 min
Total2 hr 30 min
Yield12 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 12 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil ditalini according to package directions until tender; drain and rinse with cold water until cool. Drain thoroughly.
  2. Hard-boil, cool, peel, and dice eggs.
  3. Dice cheddar cheese and sweet pickles.
  4. Combine cooled pasta, eggs, cheese, and pickles; stir in mayonnaise, starting with ½ cup.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste, cover, and refrigerate until chilled, preferably at least 2 hours.
