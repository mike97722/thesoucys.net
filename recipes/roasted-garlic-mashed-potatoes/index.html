Roasted Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Creamy Yukon Gold mashed potatoes with roasted garlic, plant butter, and unsweetened oat milk.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook45 min
Total1 hr 5 min
Yield12 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 12 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Slice tops from garlic heads, drizzle with olive oil, wrap in foil, and roast at 400°F for 35–45 minutes until soft.
  2. Peel potatoes if desired, cut evenly, and cover with cold salted water.
  3. Bring to boil and cook 15–20 minutes until fork-tender.
  4. Drain well and return briefly to warm pot to evaporate excess moisture.
  5. Squeeze roasted garlic into potatoes; mash with plant butter.
  6. Gradually add warm oat milk to preferred consistency; season with salt and pepper and garnish if desired.
