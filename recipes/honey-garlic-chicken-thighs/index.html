Honey-Garlic Chicken Thighs with Mashed Potatoes & Roasted Carrots

Juicy honey-garlic glazed chicken thighs with dairy-free mashed Yukon Gold potatoes and roasted carrots. A generous batch for family dinner and leftovers.

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss carrots with 1 tablespoon olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread on a large sheet pan and roast 25–35 minutes, turning once, until tender and caramelized.
  2. Place potatoes in a large pot, cover with cold salted water, and bring to a boil. Simmer 15–20 minutes until fork-tender.
  3. Pat chicken dry and season with salt, pepper, and smoked paprika. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken in batches for 3–4 minutes per side until golden; transfer to a plate.
  4. Whisk honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, ginger, rice vinegar, and optional red pepper flakes together. Pour into the skillet and simmer for 1–2 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
  5. Return chicken and accumulated juices to the skillet, turning to coat. Transfer skillet to the oven and roast about 10–15 minutes, until the thickest pieces reach 165°F (74°C). If your skillet is not oven-safe, move chicken and sauce to a baking dish.
  6. Move cooked chicken to a platter. Simmer the pan sauce briefly on the stovetop until slightly thickened, then spoon over chicken. Avoid reducing too far, as honey can scorch.
  7. Drain potatoes thoroughly. Mash with plant-based butter and enough warm unsweetened dairy-free milk or chicken broth to reach your preferred texture. Season to taste.
  8. Serve honey-garlic chicken with mashed potatoes and roasted carrots. Spoon extra glaze over the chicken and garnish if desired.

Recipe Notes

Be sure to use plant-based butter and unsweetened dairy-free milk or chicken broth for the mashed potatoes. The carrots and potatoes can cook while the chicken is prepared; use two oven racks if necessary.
