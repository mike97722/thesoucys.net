One-Pan Cajun Chicken & Sausage Rice

Smoky Cajun chicken thighs, sliced sausage, bell peppers and seasoned rice in one skillet.

Main DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Total45 min
Servings7 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 5 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a deep skillet or Dutch oven. Brown sliced sausage for 3–4 minutes; remove.
  2. Add chicken and 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning. Cook 4–5 minutes.
  3. Add onion and bell peppers and cook until softened, about 4 minutes. Stir in garlic.
  4. Add rice, tomatoes, broth, remaining Cajun seasoning, paprika, thyme and optional cayenne. Return sausage to pan.
  5. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook over low heat for 18–22 minutes, until rice is tender and chicken reaches 165°F. Add broth if needed.
  6. Rest covered 5 minutes. If desired, garnish with briefly sautéed green onions.

Recipe Notes

  • Choose dairy-free smoked sausage and check Cajun seasoning for allergens.
  • Adjust cayenne to control the heat.
  • Thaw chicken in the refrigerator; refrigerate leftovers within two hours.
