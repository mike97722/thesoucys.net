Bruce's Quick Candied Yams

A small batch of canned sweet potatoes baked in a buttery brown-sugar glaze without marshmallows.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Yield1 small 8×8-inch dish
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 1 batch

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Drain sweet potatoes and arrange in a lightly greased 8×8-inch baking dish.
  3. Mix melted plant butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, and optional nutmeg; spoon over potatoes.
  4. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes until hot and glazed, spooning syrup over potatoes once or twice. No marshmallows.
