Classic Deviled Eggs

Traditional deviled eggs with mayonnaise, yellow and Dijon mustard, sweet pickle juice, and paprika.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep25 min
Cook15 min
Total40 min
Yield36 halves
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 1 batch

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Cover eggs with cold water, bring to boil, remove from heat, cover, and let stand 11–12 minutes.
  2. Transfer to ice water and cool completely; peel and slice lengthwise.
  3. Remove yolks and mash with mayonnaise, both mustards, and 1 tbsp sweet pickle juice.
  4. Adjust with additional sweet pickle juice, salt, and pepper to taste.
  5. Spoon or pipe filling into egg whites, sprinkle paprika, cover, and refrigerate until serving.
