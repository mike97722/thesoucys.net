Braised Beef

Browned chunks of boneless chuck roast braised with cooked onions, garlic, red wine, and warm spices until tender.

Main DishDairy-free
Prep25 min
Cook2 hr
Total2 hr 25 min
Servings12 servings

Instructions

  1. Cut the beef into large chunks.
  2. Preheat oven to 360°F.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Brown the beef chunks, then remove and set aside.
  4. Cook the onions in the same skillet until fully cooked or translucent.
  5. In a Dutch oven, layer half the browned beef chunks, half the cumin, half the paprika, half the salt, half the black pepper, then half the sautéed onions and half the pressed garlic.
  6. Repeat the layering with the remaining beef, spices, onions, and garlic.
  7. Add red wine and water. Cover, bring to a boil on the cooktop, then transfer to the oven. Braise at 360°F for 1½–2 hours, until the beef is fully cooked and tender.
