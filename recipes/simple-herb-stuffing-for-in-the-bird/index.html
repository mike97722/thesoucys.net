Simple Herb Stuffing for in the Bird

Classic Pepperidge Farm herb-seasoned bread cubes with cooked onion, celery, plant butter, and broth, loosely stuffed inside the turkey.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep15 min
CookVariable
TotalVariable
YieldUp to 2 bags, amount depends on turkey cavity
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 1 batch

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Check package directions and prepare only as much stuffing as will fit loosely in the turkey.
  2. Sauté chopped onion and celery in plant butter until soft.
  3. Combine cooked vegetables with bread cubes and broth in the proportions on the package. Stir just until evenly moistened.
  4. Immediately stuff the turkey loosely and begin roasting; do not stuff ahead or pack tightly.
  5. Verify center of stuffing reaches 165°F. Bake any excess stuffing separately to 165°F.
