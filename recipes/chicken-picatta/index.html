Chicken Picatta

Tender chicken breast cutlets with artichoke hearts and capers in a rich white wine, garlic, and lemon sauce.

Main DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Total50 min
Servings12 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 12 servings

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Sauce

Instructions

  1. Fillet chicken breasts in two.
  2. Season flour with salt and pepper.
  3. Dredge chicken in seasoned flour.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  5. Cook chicken, working in batches as necessary, until cooked through to 165°F.
  6. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
  7. DO NOT CLEAN PAN!
  8. Add olive oil and half the plant butter to the pan.
  9. Add garlic and artichoke hearts; sauté until garlic is soft, about 1 minute.
  10. Stir in wine and deglaze pan.
  11. Bring to a simmer and allow to reduce by half, about 3 minutes.
  12. Stir in broth, lemon juice, and capers.
  13. Reduce heat to medium; simmer and whisk in remaining plant butter.
  14. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sauce thickens, about 5 minutes.
  15. Stir in parsley and adjust seasoning to taste.
  16. Return chicken to pan, turning to coat each piece with sauce.
  17. Cook just until chicken is warmed through, about 2 minutes.
  18. Serve immediately.
