Steak Fajita Rice Bowls

Citrus-marinated flank steak with sautéed peppers and fully cooked onions over seasoned rice.

Dairy-freeAlmond-freeNo raw onions
Prep20 min
Cook25 min
Total45 min
Servings7 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 7 servings

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine 2 tablespoons lime juice, garlic, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, ½ teaspoon salt, and black pepper. Rub over flank steak and let sit 15–30 minutes while preparing the rice.
  2. Rinse rice if desired. Combine rice, water or broth, tomato paste, garlic powder, and remaining ½ teaspoon salt in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and cook according to rice package directions, usually 15–18 minutes. Rest covered 5 minutes and fluff.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook peppers and onions 8–12 minutes until softened and onions are fully cooked; transfer to a bowl.
  4. Heat remaining oil in skillet or use a hot grill. Sear flank steak about 4–6 minutes per side, depending on thickness and desired doneness. Use a thermometer to check the center.
  5. Rest steak 5–10 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
  6. Build bowls with seasoned rice, cooked peppers and onions, and sliced steak. Finish with remaining tablespoon lime juice and optional cilantro, avocado, or onion-free salsa.
