Homemade Turkey Gravy

Rich turkey gravy made from a simple seasoned roux and stock, finished with strained turkey drippings.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Total30 min
Yield32 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 32 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Melt plant butter or turkey fat in large saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Whisk in flour and cook 3–5 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Gradually whisk in stock until smooth; add sage, thyme, and pepper.
  4. Simmer 10–15 minutes until thickened. Refrigerate promptly if making base ahead.
  5. Reheat thoroughly before serving; whisk in strained turkey drippings and adjust seasoning and consistency with stock.
