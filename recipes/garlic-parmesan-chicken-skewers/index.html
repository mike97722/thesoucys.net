Garlic Parmesan Chicken Skewers

Air-fried chicken skewers seasoned with garlic, Parmesan, paprika and Cajun seasoning, basted with garlic butter.

Air fryerContains dairy6 servings
Prep30 min
Cook20 min
Total50 min
Servings6 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 6 servings

Ingredients

Chicken

Garlic Butter

Preparation Steps

  1. In a bowl, toss the chicken with olive oil, Montreal chicken seasoning, grated parmesan, garlic powder, paprika, and Cajun seasoning. If using, mix in the mayo for extra juiciness.
  2. Thread the seasoned chicken pieces onto skewers.
  3. Air fry at 400°F for 10 minutes.
  4. While the chicken is cooking, mix the garlic butter ingredients together.
  5. After 10 minutes, baste the chicken with garlic butter, flip the skewers, and cook for another 10 minutes.
  6. Once done, brush on more garlic butter, sprinkle with shredded parmesan, and serve.

Recipe Notes

This recipe contains dairy from Parmesan. Ensure chicken reaches a safe internal temperature of 165°F (74°C) before serving.
