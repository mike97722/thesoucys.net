Herb-Roasted Thanksgiving Turkey

A traditional 20–22 lb herb-seasoned turkey cooked in an electric roaster with loosely packed stuffing inside.

Main DishDairy-free

Yield8–10 servings plus leftovers

Preparation Steps

  1. Refrigerator-thaw the turkey fully, allowing about 5–6 days for a frozen 20–22 lb bird. Do not wash the turkey.
  2. Remove giblets and neck. Pat dry. Mix plant butter, olive oil, and seasonings; rub over and beneath breast skin where possible.
  3. Prepare stuffing just before cooking, and fill cavities loosely. Never refrigerate a raw turkey already stuffed overnight.
  4. Preheat electric roaster following its instructions. Place turkey breast-side up on its rack and close lid.
  5. Monitor breast, innermost thigh, and stuffing center with thermometer probes. Cooking time varies by roaster and stuffing volume.
  6. Verify breast, innermost thigh and wing, and the center of the stuffing each reach at least 165°F before serving. Rest turkey 20–30 minutes, keeping stuffing safely hot.
