MawMaw’s Cranberry Sauce

A cherished family holiday cranberry sauce with fresh cranberries, cherry and lime gelatin, and undrained crushed pineapple.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Total22 min
Yield12 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 12 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Rinse cranberries and discard damaged berries.
  2. Combine cranberries, sugar, and water in saucepan; bring to a boil.
  3. Reduce heat and simmer 10–12 minutes until most berries burst.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in cherry and lime gelatin powders until completely dissolved.
  5. Stir in crushed pineapple with all its juice.
  6. Transfer to a serving dish and refrigerate until set, preferably overnight. This is the family's reconstructed version.
