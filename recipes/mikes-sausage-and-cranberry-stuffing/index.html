Mike's Sausage and Cranberry Stuffing

Mike's family-favorite baked stuffing with seasoned bread cubes, sausage, bacon, celery, and dried cranberries; no extra sage or thyme.

Side DishDairy-free
Prep20 min
Cook40 min
Total1 hr
Yield6 servings
Scale recipeOriginal recipe: 6 servings

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook sausage and chopped bacon until browned and fully cooked.
  3. Add chopped celery and cook until softened.
  4. Place seasoned bread cubes in a large bowl and gradually mix in chicken broth.
  5. Stir in cooked sausage, bacon, celery, and dried cranberries. No additional sage or thyme.
  6. Spread in a 9×13-inch baking dish and bake about 40 minutes, until golden and center reaches 165°F.
